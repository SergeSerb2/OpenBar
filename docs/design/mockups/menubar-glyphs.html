Geometry. Session rail y 3.5, 5 pt tall; weekly rail y 11, 3.5 pt tall; both 16 pt long from x 1. Fill = remaining × 16 (never thinner than the rail is tall), a 1 pt open gap, then the track at 28%. Stale dims the whole glyph to 50%. Status marks sit top-right with a 1 pt clear halo. Warning and critical never add color in the menu bar — the shorter fill carries the state. Critters (legacy, opt-in) keep their existing drawing.
